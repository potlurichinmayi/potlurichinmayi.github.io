Connect Axis Bank alerts

A small script in your Google account reads emails from alerts@axis.bank.in every 15 minutes, read-only, and adds each transaction here. Do this once, signed in to the Gmail account that receives the alerts.

  1. Open a new Apps Script project.
  2. In Project Settings (the gear), tick Show “appsscript.json” manifest file in editor.
  3. Back in the editor, open appsscript.json and replace everything in it with this:
  4. Open Code.gs and replace everything with the script. Your account id is already filled in.

  5. Optional, to file Uber rides paid to a driver under Uber: click + beside Files, choose Script, name it uber-trips and replace everything in it with this.
  6. Save, choose setup in the toolbar and press Run. Approve access; Google will call it an unverified app because it is your own script, so choose Advanced and continue.

Transactions from the last 120 days appear here within a minute, and new ones every 15 minutes after that.
